Set up once. Ten minutes.

No new app to learn — Quotify lives beside the mail you already answer.

Step 01

Connect your inbox

Make an account, then link Gmail or Outlook. The panel shows up next to your mail.

Step 02

Add your prices

Drop in the catalogue you already keep — spreadsheet, PDF, or straight from Fortnox and Visma.

Step 03

Read it and send

Adjust a line if you like, then send as PDF or straight into your reply. Every quote is saved in the panel.

Finds the request

Quotify flags the mails that are asking for a price, so they don't sit unanswered for three days.

Uses your prices

Your catalogue, your margins, your unit names. No generic market rates invented on your behalf.

Edits like a list

Change a quantity, swap a product, drop a line. Totals and VAT follow along.

Keeps the history

Every quote you've sent stays searchable in the panel — reopen one, copy it, send it again.

Your price list is yours. Full stop.

Quotify reads a mail when you open it, and stores the quote it produced. That's the whole footprint.

Your catalogue is never shared with other companies or used to train anything.
Quotes are drafts until you press send — no automatic replies, ever.
Delete your account and the catalogue and quote history go with it.
