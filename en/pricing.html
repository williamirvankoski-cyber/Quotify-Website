Questions people actually ask

What if it prices something wrong?

You'll see it before the customer does — every draft waits for your approval, and unmatched lines are flagged rather than guessed.

Does it read all my mail?

It reads the mail you open with the panel, and mails you mark as quote requests. Nothing else is stored.

Can I keep my own quote template?

Yes. Upload your letterhead and terms once; every PDF comes out in your layout.

What happens after the trial?

Nothing automatic. The panel goes read-only until you pick a plan; your quotes stay where they are.
