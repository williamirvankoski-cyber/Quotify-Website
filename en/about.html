About

Built because quoting eats the evening.

Small firms win work on how fast they answer. The answer takes an hour of looking up prices, so it waits until after dinner, and by then someone else has replied.

Quotify started as a spreadsheet macro for one plumbing firm that was losing jobs to slower, bigger competitors purely on response time. The macro turned into a panel that sits in the inbox, because that's where the requests already are — nobody needs another dashboard to log into.

It's deliberately narrow. Quotify does not do project management, time reporting or invoicing. It answers the question "what would this cost?" using your own prices, and then gets out of the way.

Your prices, never ours

We don't have a market rate database and we don't want one. The catalogue is yours.

A person always signs

No automatic replies. The tool drafts; you decide what leaves your name.

Narrow on purpose

One job, done well, in the place you already work.

Who it's for

Builders & renovation firms Electricians Plumbers & HVAC Cleaning & moving Agencies quoting projects
