Features

Four things, done properly.

Quotify isn't a quoting suite with 90 settings. It reads, it prices, it waits for you, it remembers.

It reads the request like a person would

Rooms and square metres in one mail, a bulleted list in the next, a photo of a boiler in the third. Quotify pulls out what's being asked for and shows you what it understood, in plain words, before any prices appear.

  • Swedish and English in the same inbox
  • Reads the thread, not just the last reply
  • Attachments and forwarded specs included

Priced from your catalogue, not the internet

Upload the price list you already maintain. Quotify matches each line to a real article number and shows the match — and tells you when it couldn't find one instead of inventing a figure.

  • Article numbers, units, margins, discount groups
  • Hourly rates per trade and per person
  • Re-upload any time; old quotes keep their old prices

You approve every quote

The draft sits in the panel until you've read it. Change a line, add a note about access to the stairwell, then send it as a PDF or paste it into your reply with your own wording.

  • Your letterhead, your terms, your VAT handling
  • Reply text you can edit before sending
  • No auto-send option — on purpose

Every quote, still there in March

The panel keeps the lot: what you quoted, when, for how much, and whether it turned into a job. Search by customer or address, reopen a quote, copy it for the neighbour who wants the same bathroom.
