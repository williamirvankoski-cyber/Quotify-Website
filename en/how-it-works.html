How it works

From mail to quote, in one screen.

Setup takes about ten minutes. After that the loop below is the whole product.

01

A quote request lands

You open the mail like any other. Quotify's panel is already beside it, and it has read the thread — including the two forwarded replies and the attached floor plan.

Reads
Subject, body, thread, attachments
Ignores
Newsletters, invoices, everything else
Takes
About four seconds
02

Quotify prices it

Each thing being asked for is matched against your catalogue: article number, unit, your price, your margin. Labour comes from your hourly rates. Anything it can't match is flagged rather than filled in.

03

You read it and send

Adjust anything that needs adjusting — you know the job, Quotify doesn't. Then send as a PDF attachment or drop the lines into your reply. The quote is saved with the customer, ready to reopen when they call back in October.

Sent as PDF Saved to quote history Follow-up reminder in 7 days

Connect the inbox, upload the price list, send one test quote to yourself. That's the onboarding.
